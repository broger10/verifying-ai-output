# One-page checklist

Use it before, during and after any piece of work where an AI system produces something another
person will rely on. Every box is yes or no.

## Before starting

- [ ] The brief has all seven fields: goal, input, deliverable, success criteria, constraints, out of
      scope, decisions reserved to the owner.
- [ ] Open questions went back to the owner in one batch, and were answered.
- [ ] The input is listed. Every part is mapped or declared out of scope, with the reason.
- [ ] The citation format is fixed for each kind of source (document and section, audio timestamp,
      screenshot number, web address with access date).
- [ ] The acceptance tests are written down, dated, and will not be changed after the work starts.

## Before each unit of work runs

- [ ] It has one goal, and its non-goals are written.
- [ ] Someone with no memory of the project could carry it out from this unit and the documents it
      points to.
- [ ] Stress test of the plan: approved.
- [ ] Every reference in the plan was opened and says what the plan claims: approved.

## While writing

- [ ] Every claim has a source, or is marked in the text as not traceable.
- [ ] No claim rests on "reasonable", "well known", "industry standard" or "obvious".
- [ ] Words like "only", "first", "none", "never" were checked as claims, not used as adjectives.
- [ ] Before writing "it is not there", the search was shown to find something known to be there.
- [ ] Disagreements between sources or reviewers are written down with the decision and its reason.
- [ ] Facts about real people were not decided: they were reported and passed to the owner.
- [ ] If an assumption stopped holding, the work stopped and went back to the two checks.

## Before delivering

- [ ] The reviewer is not the author.
- [ ] Sources were reopened, and the deliverable says how many out of how many.
- [ ] The share of content marked as not traceable is stated. If it is above the agreed threshold, the
      owner was told.
- [ ] If a length limit and the markings conflicted, content was cut and the markings stayed.
- [ ] Counts and cross-references that appear in more than one document agree with each other
      (the review checks do not catch this: check it by hand).
- [ ] The final tests were run by someone who saw only the result.
- [ ] The limits are written at the end: what was not checked, and where it could be wrong.
- [ ] The result went to the owner. Sending it is the owner's decision.

## After delivering

- [ ] Every error found became a written rule for next time.
